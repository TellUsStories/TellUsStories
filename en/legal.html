Terms of sale

What we sell

Personalised illustrated books: we write and illustrate a story based on the story and photos you send us, and we print it and send it to the address you give us.

Price and payment

  • Paperback: €43.50. Hardback: €61.50. Shipping to Spain: €5.95. This is the final price (book + shipping): there are no other charges.
  • Payment is made by card through Stripe, a secure payment platform. We never see or store your card details.
  • We start preparing the book once we receive the payment.

Delivery times

Preparing, printing and sending the book usually takes between 2 and 3 weeks from payment. If there is any delay, we will let you know by email.

Review of the book

Before printing, we review the text and the illustrations. If we need to clarify anything about your story, we will write to you. The dedication is printed exactly as you wrote it.

Withdrawal and returns

Each book is made to order with your story and your photos. For this reason, under article 103.c of the Spanish General Law for the Protection of Consumers and Users, the 14-day right of withdrawal does not apply.

If the book arrives damaged, with a printing defect or with a mistake on our part, write to us at hello@taleusstories.com within 14 days of receiving it, with a photo, and we will reprint it and send it again at no cost, or refund your money.

If you change your mind

If you write to us before we start writing your book, we will cancel the order and refund the full amount.

Complaints

For any question or complaint, write to us at hello@taleusstories.com and we will reply as soon as possible. You can also ask for official complaint forms. Spanish law applies.

Privacy policy

Who processes your data

Cristina Casanovas (Tale Us Stories), with the contact details given in the legal notice.

What data we collect

  • Your contact details: name, email and phone number.
  • The shipping address.
  • The story you tell us (text and voice notes), the dedication and the photos you upload, which may include children and other family members.
  • Payment data (amount and status). Card details are processed only by Stripe.

When you send us photos or data of other people, such as children, you confirm that you have permission to do so (for example, because you are their mother, father or guardian, or because they have given it to you).

What we use it for

  • To write, illustrate, print and send you your book, and to talk to you about your order. The legal basis is the contract you enter into with us when you order the book.
  • To comply with legal obligations, such as invoicing.

We do not sell your data. Your story, your photos and your contact details are never used for advertising. We will not publish your book or your photos anywhere without asking your permission first.

Who we share it with

Only with the services we need to make your book, which act on our instructions:

  • Cloudflare (website hosting).
  • Make (automates the order).
  • Google (email and order storage).
  • OpenAI (turns your voice notes into text and helps write the story).
  • Artificial intelligence illustration tools and layout tools (they create the illustrations and the pages of the book).
  • Stripe (card payment).
  • Meta (Instagram and Facebook), only if you accept cookies: to know which ads work. See Cookies.
  • Gelato (prints the book and sends it to your address).

Some of these services are outside the European Economic Area, mainly in the United States. In those cases, transfers are protected by the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses or by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

How long we keep it

The photos, voice notes and story are kept while we prepare the book and for up to 6 months after sending it, in case it needs to be reprinted. After that, we delete them. Invoicing data is kept for as long as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data, to limit its use, to object to its use or to take it with you. To do so, write to hello@taleusstories.com. If you believe we have not handled it properly, you can file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).

Cookies

If you click “Accept” on the cookie notice, we use the Meta pixel (Instagram and Facebook). It helps us know how many people arrive from our ads, how many start or send the form and how many buy, and to show our ads to people who may be interested. Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd. receives this information together with the cookies of its service; you can read how it handles it in its privacy policy. The legal basis is your consent, and if you click “Reject”, none of this is activated.

You can change your choice at any time: change my cookie preferences.

To know where each visit comes from (for example, Google, Instagram or an ad), your browser saves a note on your own device with the website or link of origin. It does not include personal data, and we send it with the order only if you place one.

To know how many people visit the website and which pages they read, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics, an anonymous statistics service that does not use cookies, does not store anything on your device and does not follow you across other websites.

While you fill in the form, your browser saves a draft so that you don’t lose what you have written if you close the page. It stays only on your device and is deleted when you send the order.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. When you pay, Stripe may use its own cookies to keep the payment secure and to prevent fraud.
