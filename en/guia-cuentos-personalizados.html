Personalised storybooks: types, prices and how to choose one

A personalised storybook is an illustrated book created for someone in particular and printed to order. It is one of the most sought-after gifts for children, for mums and dads and for grandparents, but they are not all the same: some only change the name, others change the main character’s looks, and others tell a real story from beginning to end. Here we explain the differences so you can choose well.

The three types of personalised storybook

1. With the child’s name

The story is already written and the book changes the main character’s name, sometimes the dedication too. They are quick to make and are usually the cheapest. They work very well with the youngest children, who light up when they hear their name read aloud.

2. With the child’s looks or photo

As well as the name, the main character looks like the child: you choose the colour of their hair and skin, or whether they wear glasses, or their photo is used. The story is still the same for everyone, but the child recognises themselves on every page.

3. With a real story

Here an existing story is not adapted: a new one is written from real memories. It can tell how the child was born, how their parents met, or the life of their mum, dad or grandparents before the child arrived. It is the most moving type and the one people keep the most, because nobody else has that book.

How to choose a good personalised storybook

  • Think about what you want them to feel. If you are after a quick smile, one with their name works. If you want them to be moved and to keep it, a real story is better.
  • Look at the format. The size, the paper and the cover make a difference in a book that will be read many times. A hardback stands up better to the passing years.
  • Pay attention to the illustrations. They should have a careful, consistent style on every page, not just on the cover.
  • Ask whether you will be able to check it. Before it is printed, it is important to see the text and check the names, dates and dedication. We explain this in made-to-order book or generated on the spot.
  • Work out the timing. A made-to-order book cannot be thrown together in two days. For Christmas or a birthday, order it in good time.
  • Check what happens to your photos. They should only be used to make the book.

Who is it a good idea for?

For children, of course, but more and more are given to adults: to a mum who wants her children to know what she was like when she was young, to grandparents celebrating a birthday or retiring, or to the whole family at Christmas. Often the book is ordered by the children or grandchildren together, as a joint gift.

Frequently asked questions

What is a personalised storybook?

It is an illustrated book created for one particular person: it can carry the child’s name, their looks or their photo, or tell a real story from their family. It is printed to order, one copy at a time.

How much does a personalised storybook cost?

Books that change the name and looks of the main character in an already written story usually cost between 25 and 45 euros. Books written and illustrated to order from a real story cost a little more, because each one is unique. At Tale Us Stories they cost €43.50 in paperback and €61.50 in hardback, plus €5.95 shipping.

How long does a personalised storybook take to arrive?

If the story is already written and only the name changes, it usually arrives in one or two weeks. If it is written and illustrated to order, allow two or three weeks. For Christmas, it is worth ordering in good time.

What age is a personalised storybook for?

Books with the child’s name and looks work best from 0 to 7. Books that tell the real story of their mum, dad or grandparents are enjoyed from age 3 and are reread for a lifetime, into adulthood too.

Can you make a personalised storybook about an adult?

Yes. It is what we do at Tale Us Stories: the real life of a mum, dad, grandparents or aunts and uncles told as a story for the children, from their childhood to today, with their real features drawn from a photo.
