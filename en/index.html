About us

Hello, I’m Cris.

A while ago I realised something. My friends, incredible women, had become above all "mums"Carlota, María, Ana. And sometimes even they had forgotten the girl who travelled alone, who dreamt big or who laughed until she cried.

And it doesn’t only happen to them. It happens to dads, to grandparents, to aunts and uncles… to everyone who one day became, above all, a label.

That’s why Tale Us Stories was born: so that children can discover who that person was before they became everything to them. And so that, as they read it, that person recognises themselves again.

Personalised storybooks

A personalised storybook that tells a real life.

Most personalised storybooks put the child’s name into a made-up story. Ours do the opposite: they tell the real story of someone the children love, so they can truly get to know them.

It’s a personalised book for grandparents, for mum, for dad, for aunts and uncles or godparents, or with your own story, written from their memories: where they grew up, what they dreamt of, how they fell in love, how each grandchild arrived and what they want them to know when they grow up. Every page is illustrated in watercolour style with their real features, based on a photo.

That’s why it’s a personalised gift that doesn’t end up in a drawer: for Mother’s Day, Father’s Day or Grandparents’ Day, for a retirement, for the birth of a grandchild or as a memory book in memory of someone who is no longer here.

Frequently asked questions

What you ask us most.

Any other questions? Write to us on Instagram.

What is Tale Us Stories?

Tale Us Stories is a Barcelona brand that creates personalised storybooks with real stories. Each one is a book illustrated in watercolour style that tells the real life of someone special to the children (mum, dad, the grandparents, an aunt or an uncle) from their childhood to today, written from their memories and with their real features. That way the children discover who they were before they became “Mum” or “Grandad”.

How much does a personalised storybook cost?

€43.50 in paperback and €61.50 in hardback, plus €5.95 shipping.

Who can I make the book for?

For anyone who is special to the children: mum, dad, the grandparents, an aunt or an uncle, a godparent… It can also be about you, telling your own story for your children or grandchildren, or in memory of someone who is no longer here.

Do I have to fill in the form all at once?

No. It saves itself, so you can do it a bit at a time and at your own pace. You answer with voice notes, as if you were telling a friend, and we transcribe them.

What if I don’t have photos?

We draw them from how you describe them to us. With a photo of their face, they’ll look much more like themselves.

What is the difference between paperback and hardback?

Both are 21 × 28 cm and have the same watercolour-style illustrations on quality paper. The paperback has a flexible cover and 25 pages of story. The hardback has a rigid cover, sturdier to keep for a lifetime, and one more page of story. Home delivery costs €5.95 in both cases.

How long does it take to arrive?

Between 2 and 3 weeks from when you send us the story.

What do you do with the photos and memories?

We only use them to create your book. We never publish them or use them for anything else without your permission.
